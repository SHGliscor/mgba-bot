Pokebot3DS-CFW Gen 3 Starter Game Profiles — v0p21

Supported English ROMs
----------------------
Ruby:
  AXVE rev 00 = v1.0
  AXVE rev 01 = v1.1

Sapphire:
  AXPE rev 00 = v1.0
  AXPE rev 01 = v1.1

Detection
---------
PB3 GAME selects Ruby vs Sapphire.
ROM header byte 0x080000BC selects revision 00 vs 01.

Revision-specific starter callbacks
-----------------------------------
rev 00 / v1.0:
  Task_StarterChoose2  0x0810A178
  CB2_ChooseStarter    0x08109E80

rev 01 / v1.1:
  Task_StarterChoose2  0x0810A198
  CB2_ChooseStarter    0x08109EA0

Other ROM-address differences exist between Ruby/Sapphire for some field-message
functions, but those are not used by the starter loop.
