Pokebot3DS-CFW mGBA Ruby Starter UI v0p19
==================================================

Support-bundle diagnosis from 2026-09-15 16:02:
- RTC itself was healthy and LIVE.
- 19 valid encounters had 0 exact generation duplicates.
- The v0p18 ±64 LCG-distance guard became over-broad:
  4,660 frames blocked, 4,600 of them by the neighbourhood rule.
- Attempts 16, 17, 25 and 26 timed out waiting for a frame outside the guard.
- Attempt 24 had one STARTER_OPEN acknowledgement timeout.
- The hunt safety-stopped after attempts 24-26.
- Throughput fell to ~358.5 effective rolls/hour.
- STARTER_RESULT's 192-byte response buffer also truncated CAPACITY=32768
  to values such as 3/3276 in some telemetry replies.

v0p19 changes:
- Removed the ±64 confirmation-state distance guard.
- Kept exact confirmation-state uniqueness.
- After every validated PK3, the PC reconstructs the actual Ruby
  generation_pre_rng and records it to the CIA with STARTER_RECORD.
- Before confirming the next starter, the CIA predicts generation_pre_rng
  across PID-call offsets 48-72 and blocks only a predicted repeat.
- The generation window auto-expands if hardware tracing reports an offset
  outside the current range.
- STARTER_RESULT buffer increased to 512 bytes.
- RTC/RNG tab now exposes confirm states, recorded generation states,
  exact-confirm blocks, predicted-generation blocks, guard window,
  trace coverage, PID call index, Confirm->PID calls, and generation pre-RNG.
- Existing current UI, sprites, encounter history, Recent Shinies, Random
  starter fix, and continuous phase timer are retained.

Policy:
- No RNG RAM writes.
- No RTC writes.
- No artificial reseeding.
- Live retail-style RTC remains authoritative.
