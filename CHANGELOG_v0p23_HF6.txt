Pokebot3DS-CFW Gen 3 v0p23 HF6 — Wild Intro A Fix

Hardware issue fixed:
- Wild Spin entered and decoded the encounter but stopped on
  "Wild <Pokémon> appeared!" because no A was sent before waiting for the
  Fight/Bag/Pokémon/Run menu.

HF6 changes:
1. A failed D-pad action-menu proof now establishes that the 2x2 menu is not
   interactive at that instant.
2. Only after that proof, FAST is forced to 0 and A may be sent to advance the
   wild-intro text.
3. The worker probes again before every additional intro A, so a newly-opened
   Fight menu cannot accidentally receive A.
4. Intro A is bounded to three presses and separately logged as
   WILD_INTRO_A_SENT.
5. Shiny encounters still hold before run-away logic, so no intro A is sent on
   a shiny hold.
6. Starter backend/UI/CIA remain frozen and byte-for-byte unchanged.
