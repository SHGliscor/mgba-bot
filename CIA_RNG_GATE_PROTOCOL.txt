Pokebot3DS-CFW mGBA Ruby v0p19 generation-aware RNG gate
========================================================

PB3 STARTER_ARM <seed_hex> <delay_frames>
  Arms a one-frame local A confirmation.
  Rejects an exact confirmation gRngValue already used this hunt.
  Also predicts future generation_pre_rng states for PID-call offsets in the
  current guard window (default 48-72) and rejects only a candidate that would
  reproduce an actual generation state recorded previously.

PB3 STARTER_RECORD <generation_pre_rng_hex> <confirm_to_pid_calls>
  Called by the PC after a checksum-valid PK3 is decoded and traced.
  Adds the actual CreateBoxMon generation_pre_rng to the CIA session history.
  Idempotent and safe to retry.

PB3 STARTER_RESULT
  Reports exact confirmation blocks, predicted-generation blocks, recorded
  generation count, current PID-call window, seed/attempt counters, frame
  count, and capacity.

PB3 STARTER_CLEAR
  Clears confirmation + actual-generation histories once at new-hunt start.

PB3 STARTER_CANCEL
  Cancels only the currently armed confirmation.

No game RAM writes are performed.
